About The Foundation

Creating Sustainable Impact Through Youth Leadership

InAmigos Foundation is a youth-centric non-governmental organization working relentlessly to bring equitable development to vulnerable communities.

We focus on grassroots interventions that address fundamental socio-economic challenges. By engaging student volunteers and young professionals, we bridge the gap between resources and marginalized populations, nurturing a compassionate generation of future changemakers.

🎯

Our Mission

To uplift underprivileged communities by providing access to holistic education, essential care, hunger relief, and environmental conservation.

👁️

Our Vision

A self-reliant, inclusive, and compassionate society where every individual thrives with dignity, equality, and opportunity.

Core Initiatives

Our Ongoing Projects

Targeted social interventions addressing critical community needs.

Hunger & Relief

Project SEVA

Dedicated to hunger mitigation and basic needs support. Volunteers organize nutritional food drives, warm clothes distribution, and hygiene kit delivery to homeless individuals and daily wage earners.

Education

Project Bachpanshala

An open-air educational initiative bringing basic literacy, numerical skills, and creative learning workshops directly to children living in slum clusters and rural pockets.

Empowerment

Project Udaan

Focuses on youth development, vocational skill building, and menstrual health awareness. We conduct workshops providing free sanitary pads and health education to underprivileged girls.

Animal Welfare

Project Jeev

Extends compassion to stray animals. Through daily feeding programs, water bowl installations during summer, reflective collar drives, and basic medical first-aid for strays.

Environment

Project Prakriti

Our environmental action wing organizing native tree plantation drives, plastic clean-ups, seed-ball making sessions, and environmental literacy campaigns in schools and public spaces.

Development

Project Vikas

A holistic rural and suburban outreach model aimed at setting up community health check-up camps, infrastructure improvements, and clean water awareness programs.
